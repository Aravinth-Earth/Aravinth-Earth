About QR Codes

QR codes (Quick Response codes) were invented in 1994 by Denso Wave. They can store various types of information and are widely used for contactless information sharing.

Offline Capability

This tool works completely offline once loaded. It uses Progressive Web App technology to cache all necessary resources on your device.

Privacy & Security
