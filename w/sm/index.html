How to Play

Watch the pattern of flashing colors, then repeat it by tapping the tiles in the same order.

  • Watch — the computer plays a sequence of colored tiles
  • Repeat — tap the tiles in the exact same order
  • Each round adds one new step to the sequence
  • Keyboard — press 1 2 3 4 for the tiles
