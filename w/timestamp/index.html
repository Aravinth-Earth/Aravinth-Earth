Format details

YYYYMMDDHHMMSSZZZ adds milliseconds (000–999), the SAP convention. YYYYMMDDHHMMSS stops at seconds.

YYYY
Four-digit year
MM
Month, 01–12
DD
Day, 01–31
HH
Hour, 00–23
MM
Minute, 00–59
SS
Second, 00–59
ZZZ
Milliseconds, 000–999
